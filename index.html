Hi, I'm Austin

I make apps and games on my own. Here's what you can expect from every one of them.

  • FreeFree to download and play. There's an optional tip jar if you want to support me, but tips don't unlock anything.
  • No adsNone. Not even a banner.
  • No trackingNo analytics, and nothing that follows you around other apps or websites.
  • No data collectionI don't collect anything about you or how you play.
  • Stays on your deviceYour data lives on your phone. Optional extras like Game Center and Maps are run by Apple.
  • Respects your time and moneyNo timers to wait out, and nothing nagging you to spend money.

If any of this ever changes for an app, I'll say so on its App Store page and in the privacy policy before the update goes out.

How I make them

Unless an app's credits say otherwise, the ideas, gameplay, art, and sound are all mine.

About AI. I use AI coding tools to help write some of the code, mostly game logic. I read, test, and stand behind everything that ships.

I don't use AI for art or sound. Those are human things to me, and I won't knowingly use anyone else's assets that were made with AI either.

If I use someone else's work, like a font or a sound effect, they get credit in the app.
