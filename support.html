Common questions

Is my progress backed up?

Your progress is saved on your device. It's included in your iCloud or device backups, but if you delete the app, its data goes with it. Some apps, like Plate Atlas, also let you export your data to a file.

Why are your apps free with no ads?

Like many of you, I enjoy playing mobile games, but they're often plagued with tracking, ads, data collection, or pressure to wait or spend money to keep playing. I want my games to stand out from that and reflect better values.

Do I need Game Center?

Nope. In apps that support it, Game Center adds leaderboards and achievements, but you can play everything without it.

What do tips get me?

Nothing extra in the game. Everything is available without tipping. Tips are just a way to say thanks if you want to.

Can I get a refund on a tip?

Apple handles all purchases, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Some apps, like Plate Atlas, also have a delete option inside the app. I don't collect anything, so there's nothing on my end to delete.
